FADE IN:

## ext. ridge road - night

Rain hammers a gravel switchback high above a hollow. A pickup truck idles with one headlight out. Its cab glows with the green light of a dashboard radio.

## INT. PICKUP TRUCK - CONTINUOUS

MARGARET HALE (60s), wiry, in a canvas coat two sizes too big, turns the radio dial a hair at a time. Static. More static.

Then, under it: a voice.

**Old Tom** (V.O.)
*(faint, distorted)*
Margaret. You still listening?

Margaret freezes. Her hand drops from the dial.

**MARGARET**
That's not possible.

She grabs the handset off its hook and keys it.

**MARGARET:** *(into the handset)* Tom? Tom, if that's you, say something only you would know.

Static. Then the voice again, steadier now.

**OLD TOM** (V.O.)
The well behind the church. You dropped your mother's ring down it the summer we were twelve, and you made me swear I'd never tell her. I never did. Not when she asked, not when she was dying, not once. I kept it the way I kept everything you ever gave me to keep. You know that. You've always known that. That's why you're up on this ridge in the rain at two in the morning with a dead man's radio, isn't it? Because some part of you never stopped believing I'd find a way back to the frequency. Well, I did. And I don't have long, so I need you to listen to me carefully. There's something down in Shaft Four that wasn't there when we were kids. The company knew. They sealed it in 'seventy-two and they told everyone it was black damp. It wasn't. It was never the air. Go to the old company store. Third floorboard from the stove. Everything you need is there. Don't take Route Nine. Don't trust the sheriff. And whatever you do, Margaret, don't answer if it calls you by your full name.

The signal dies. Margaret stares at the handset.

> CUT TO:

## EXT. ABANDONED COMPANY STORE - DAWN

Gray light. Kudzu has swallowed the porch. A rusted sign: **CASH ONLY - NO SCRIP**.

The pickup rolls to a stop. Margaret climbs out, flashlight in hand.

## INT. COMPANY STORE - CONTINUOUS

Dust hangs in the air. A pot-bellied stove squats in the corner.

### ON THE FLOOR

Margaret counts the boards from the stove. One. Two. Three. She kneels and pries the third one up with a screwdriver.

Underneath: a tin box, wrapped in oilcloth.

**MARGARET**
*(to herself)*
Alright, Tom.

She opens it. Inside, a stack of yellowed survey maps and a reel-to-reel tape labeled in neat block capitals: <u>DO NOT PLAY UNDERGROUND</u>.

Behind her, the front door creaks open.

**VOICE** (O.S.)
Margaret Ann Hale.

She doesn't turn around.
